ENGINEERING TIME

Watch Technology

Understanding the systems that keep modern and traditional watches moving.

⚙

Automatic Movement

Automatic watches use a mechanical movement powered by the motion of the wearer. A rotor winds the mainspring as the watch is worn.

◌

Quartz Precision

Quartz watches use an electronic oscillator regulated by a quartz crystal, offering reliable timekeeping and efficient battery use.

☀

Eco-Friendly Solar

Solar-powered watch systems convert available light into electrical energy, reducing the need for frequent battery replacement.

⌁

Smart Connectivity

Smart watches combine sensors, displays and wireless connectivity to provide notifications, activity information and useful digital features.

WATCH CARE

Technology Needs Care

Regular servicing can help maintain performance. Keep mechanical watches clean and dry, follow the manufacturer's water-resistance guidance, and have complex repairs handled by qualified technicians.
